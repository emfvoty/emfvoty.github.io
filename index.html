EMF Village of the Year 2028

An opt-in competition to identify the very best village, the most exemplary individual categories, and the finest civic efforts of 2028. The small complication is that, owing to budget reductions, the only overall award this year is Worst Village, which of course requires an altogether different and deeply committed kind of excellence.

Introduction

It has come to our attention, via recently rediscovered footage from the 2017 Bodgeham-on-Wye Primary School Year 7 "EMF History Documentary" project, that the title of 2016's Worst Village may have been awarded in a wholly unacceptable manner.

The footage suggests that, rather than arising from a standards-based audit, the title was conferred by a gentleman from foreign shores wandering into the village and solemnly declaring, "This is undoubtedly the worst village of 2016" without any methodology, scoring framework, or judging criteria whatsoever.

We find this entirely unacceptable and offer our wholehearted apologies for this egregious historical oversight.

To rectify matters, we will host the 2028 EMF Village of the Year competition, where our team of expert judges and auditors will assess architecture, aesthetics, functionality, health and safety, process management, and a multitude of other categories against the as-yet-unratified EMFSO-4242 Village Compliance Standards. This time, judging will be afforded the respect and seriousness it deserves.

Funding Note: due to parish council budget reductions, the only overall category this year is Worst Village. Discretionary awards may still be issued for distinctions including Best Non-Functioning Technology, Commitment to Process Avoidance, and Best Over-Ambitious Failure.

Worst Village

The Worst Village is awarded to the village which shows just how far it's possible to deviate from the standard. It must show disregard for process, and not attention to detail whatsoever.

Special Awards

The panel may issue additional commendations where standards of (under)performance are notably advanced. E.g.

  • Most Ambitious Queue for the Simplest Thing
  • Best Improvised Explanation of Why It Definitely Worked Yesterday
  • Outstanding Contribution to Non-Operational Tech
  • The "It's not dumb if it works" award for the most inappropriate use of technology to solve a simple problem
  • Lifetime Achievement in "It Seemed Like a Good Idea at the Time"

Note: potential category names are reviewed annually in response to emerging incidents and deeply avoidable circumstances.
